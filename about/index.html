Hello, my name is Adron Hall.  I’ve studied transit (and have even been paid a few times for consulting), the history of, the economics behind it, politics, and much more. This has included primarily transit and transportation in the United States, but has not excluded systems worldwide. Over the years I’ve been blogging I kind of gave myself and picked up the moniker of “Transit Sleuth“.  I named my blog that some bunch of years ago and just left it at that.  I like it and it seems to work, so welcome to my writings. I hope you enjoy the writings, ramblings, & research.

For more specifics about me, check out my disclaimer and manifesto.
